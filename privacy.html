Privacy

Ultimate Prompt Architect is a static browser app. It does not require an account, backend server, database, analytics script, or payment processor.

Saved snapshots are stored in your browser localStorage on your device. Clearing browser data may delete saved snapshots. Anything you paste into the app stays in the browser unless you copy it, download it, or submit it somewhere else yourself.

The app can register a service worker so the page loads faster and can work offline after the first visit. This cache contains app files only.
